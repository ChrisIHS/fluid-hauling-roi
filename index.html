Data note Trip counts are a portfolio-level approximation on a uniform tank size. Very high water-cut wells may fill a standard tank in under a day — those wells likely warrant well-specific tank sizing or continuous disposal rather than this shared assumption.
Methodology: Trip count = 365 × daily rate ÷ (tank capacity × trigger%) × loads/visit, calculated independently for oil and water tanks. Loads/visit = ceil(pickup volume ÷ tanker capacity) — if the volume in the tank at the pickup trigger exceeds a single truckload, the visit requires multiple truck loads, which multiplies the trip count (and any per-trip billing) accordingly. Under metered billing, the annual hauling invoice is fixed at (oil + water m³/d) × 365 × rate — the trigger% changes trip count only, not cost. Under flat fee per trip, each truck visit is billed at the entered flat-fee amount regardless of actual fill, so raising the trigger% reduces both trips and total spend proportionally. Under hourly + minimum, each trip bills max(hours per trip, minimum hours) × hourly rate + mobilization, plus a separate $/m³ disposal charge on actual volume — trips drive the hourly/mobilization portion down, while the disposal portion stays fixed, same as metered. Under $/m³ + wait time, each trip bills wait time × hourly rate, plus a separate $/m³ disposal charge on actual volume — trips drive the wait-time portion down, while the disposal portion stays fixed. 1 bbl = 0.158987 m³.
